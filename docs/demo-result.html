Entry point and data flow

  1. tools/demo.py copies the fixture into a new local workspace and drives the installed CLI.
  2. intake_review.py dispatches each command. Store.intake converts the Markdown and saves a version; Store.review reads that exact version; Store.decide binds the decision to its content hash.
  3. Store.export checks current source bytes, version and decision integrity, then writes JSONL under the workspace's exports/ directory.
